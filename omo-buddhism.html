  • Little Buddha (1993): Bernardo Bertolucci’s drama intertwining the life of Siddhartha Gautama with a modern search for his reincarnation.
  • Kundun (1997): Martin Scorsese’s biographical drama about the early life of the 14th Dalai Lama.
  • The Cup (1999): Khyentse Norbu’s film about young Tibetan monks obsessed with the 1998 World Cup.
  • Zen (2009): A biographical drama about Dogen Zenji, founder of the Soto school of Zen Buddhism.
  • Samsara (2011): A non-narrative documentary exploring the cycle of rebirth across various cultures.
6.4.2.2.3. Top Hindi-Language Films:
  • Tathagatha Buddha (2007): A Hindi biographical film detailing the life of Siddhartha Gautama.
  • A Journey of Samyak Buddha (2013): Directed by Praveen Damle, based on Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar’s The Buddha and His Dhamma.
  • Sri Siddhartha Gautama (2013): A Sri Lankan Hindi-dubbed film focusing on the prince’s spiritual journey.
6.4.2.2.4. Other Notable Mentions:
  • The Buddha (2010): A documentary produced by David Grubin covering the Four Noble Truths and Eightfold Path.
  • Buddha: The Great Departure (2011): An anime film based on Osamu Tezuka’s manga.
  • Siddhartha (1972): A drama based on Hermann Hesse’s novel, featuring a journey of self-discovery.
  • Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter… and Spring (2003): A Korean film often cited for its Buddhist themes of cycles and karma.
  • Travellers and Magicians (2003): A Bhutanese film exploring Buddhist concepts through a young man’s journey to America.
6.4.2.2.5. Misc
  • Lists such as those from Dharmapedia Wiki or Stanford’s Ho Center typically cap at around 10–15 significant films rather than 50.
